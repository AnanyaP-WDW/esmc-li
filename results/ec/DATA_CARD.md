## Protected splits

- S1/S2 are the FuncSeek 90:10 split of the CARE training universe, stratified by EC4, EC4 classes with <2 members removed, RNG `numpy.random.default_rng(42)`.
- S1 carries **only the first EC** per protein. Do not claim multi-functional performance from S1 alone; use `care_promiscuous.tsv` or rebuild labels from UniProt.
- Care files are the official CARE test splits redistributed by FuncSeek.
