## Thesis

Exact late-interaction retrieval over a fine-tuned ESM-C 300M scorer is
practical on a laptop at 10^5 proteins and batch-usable at 10^6, and it keeps a
sensitivity advantage over MMseqs2 and pooled ESM-C on full-length chains. The
binding constraints are index build and storage, not query search at 10^5.

## Cut from the paper

- **No Rust claim.** The systems artifact is `.esmci` + numpy/tiled GEMM + a
  fused Metal kernel. There is no Rust implementation.
- **No DHR / TM-Vec / DeepBLAST / PLMSearch / pLM-BLAST / ERAST / Foldseek
  head-to-head numbers.** They were not run. Cite them as related work; do not
  compare published numbers across different splits/metrics.
- **No Pfam retrieval result.** Pfam data was fetched but not evaluated.
- **No identity thresholds below 40%.** SCOPe is ASTRAL 40%; report the maximum
  train/test identity over >=80%-coverage alignments (38.2%) and say so.
- **No 10M / 100M result.** Only 10K/100K/1M are measured. Anything beyond is
  extrapolation and must be labelled as such.
- **No "first" claim.**
- **No claim to the model idea.** ProtoCol introduced residue-level late
  interaction for protein homolog search. This paper contributes the
  deployment-shaped benchmark, ESM-C training study, index/search systems, and
  software.
