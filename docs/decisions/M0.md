# Gate M0 — decision

**Result.** Harness frozen. Reproduced ProtoCol Table 1 (SCOPe, n=2314) on our
split: trained cR@10 0.8933 vs 0.8947 published (Δ=-0.0014), frozen 0.8096 vs
0.8093 (Δ=+0.0003). MinHash cR@10 0.1956 vs 0.1932 (Δ=+0.0024), MMseqs2
0.6179 vs 0.6177 (Δ=+0.0002) — all « 0.05, no stop condition.

**Decision.** PASS. M1 may start; `esmc_li/eval.py` + split hashes
(commit 4508ab1) are frozen and all later numbers must come from them.

**Why / notes.**
- ProtoCol's *released code* defaults (`-e 0.001 --max-seqs 101`) give MMseqs2
  cR@10 0.497, not the paper's 0.618; the paper matches `-e 1e10 --max-seqs
  1000` (Δ≤0.0003). We adopt the paper settings as the MMseqs config.
- Leakage: max train↔test identity over ≥80%-coverage alignments is 38.2%
  (SCOPe) / 32.8% (Pfam); the raw 100% hits are a 17-column local match and a
  contained Pfam fragment. Recorded in results/m0_split_identity.txt.
- Deviations from ProtoCol's env: transformers 5.17 (pin 4.46.3 has no
  py3.13 wheels), DEVICE=mps, DataLoader num_workers=0 (macOS spawn cannot
  pickle their closure collate_fn). No numerical effect at the 0.002 level.
